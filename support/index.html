Race to 270 — Support

Support for Race to 270, an offline iPhone strategy game by Keshav Garg.

Email: khakhibuzz@gmail.com

Getting started

Tap Play, choose your candidate and difficulty, and tap Let's Campaign. The first campaign includes a guided tour. The question-mark button replays it. US game rules explain budgets, issue cards, regional networks and electoral votes.

Saving and resuming

Campaigns save automatically after each move. Tap Home and then Resume to continue. There is one campaign slot; starting a new campaign replaces it. Saves remain local to the device and cannot be recovered by the developer. Device backups are controlled by your Apple settings.

Playing offline

All gameplay works without internet access. Optional research links open external websites and need a connection. No account is needed, and there are no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Sound and haptics

Open Settings from the home screen to change sound and haptic preferences. If you hear no sound, check Silent Mode, device volume and audio output. The app respects Silent Mode. Haptics also depend on your iPhone and system settings.

Unexpected behavior

Close and reopen the app, then use Resume. If a saved campaign cannot be read, the game shows a recovery notice. Starting a new campaign replaces that save; deleting the app removes its local data. Contact support before deleting it if you want help diagnosing a problem.

Contact support

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, the screen/round where the problem occurred, and steps to reproduce it. Screenshots are helpful if they contain no private information. Do not send passwords or account credentials. The developer does not receive gameplay automatically.
