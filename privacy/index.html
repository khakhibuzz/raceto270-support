Race to 270 — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026. Publisher: Keshav Garg.

Race to 270 is an offline, single-player strategy game. The application does not collect or transmit personal information, gameplay data, device identifiers, or location data to its developer or to third parties.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your current campaign, simulated results, election-night progress, and sound/haptic preferences locally so that you can resume playing. This data is not sent to an app-operated server. Standard operating-system device backups may include app data according to your device settings.

Starting a new campaign replaces the previous campaign. Removing the app removes its locally stored data. Device backups are managed through your operating-system settings.

The app also stores whether you have seen the introductory tour. Offloading the app can retain its data; choose Delete App to remove local app data.

Accounts, advertising and tracking

The app has no accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking, in-app purchases, or third-party runtime services. No permission to access contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or location is requested.

Platform services

Apple may process information through the App Store, device backups, or system diagnostics under Apple's own policies and your device settings. Those services are separate from the game's own data practices.

External websites

Optional source and research links open third-party websites in your browser. Opening a link sends a normal web request to that website, which may process information under its own privacy policy. The game does not attach your saved campaign or preferences to these links. Gameplay does not require opening them.

Support email

If you choose to email support, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you send. This information is used to respond to your request and troubleshoot the issue, not for advertising. Messages are handled through Gmail and retained only as needed for support and applicable obligations. You can request deletion by emailing the same address. Do not send passwords or other sensitive information. The game does not send support emails or attach gameplay data automatically.

This website

These support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no analytics, advertising, forms or tracking cookies. GitHub may process technical information, such as your IP address and request logs, to deliver and secure the pages under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website hosting is separate from the offline app.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact Keshav Garg at khakhibuzz@gmail.com. Visit the support page.
